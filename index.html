Privacy Policy

Starship Survivors — Last updated: May 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Starship Survivors ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name and email address. This is used solely to identify your account and save your game progress.

Game data
We store your scores, upgrades, daily run results, and in-game currency (flux) on our servers so your progress is preserved across devices.

Device information (feedback and bug reports only)
If you submit feedback or a bug report, we collect: device model and screen resolution, operating system, browser/app user agent, CPU core count, approximate RAM, GPU renderer name, and display language. This information is used only to help diagnose issues.

Anonymous device identifier
A randomly generated ID is stored locally on your device to prevent duplicate feedback submissions. It is not linked to your account or personal identity.

Advertising (Android only)
The app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You are never required to watch an ad to play the game.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save and sync your game progress
  • To display leaderboards
  • To deliver optional rewards when you choose to watch an ad
  • To diagnose bugs and improve the game (feedback data only)

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy (Google Sign-In, AdMob).

3. Data Storage

Game data is stored on servers provided by Supabase, located in the European Union. Some preferences are also stored locally on your device using standard browser storage.

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Feedback and bug report submissions are stored indefinitely for development purposes and contain no personally identifiable information beyond what you choose to write.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Deleting the app removes locally stored data from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

8. Account & Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us at soldanrobertandrei@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 30 days.

Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data immediately.
